# Toxicity
A bit of heat is expected in gang RP — tension, ego, and some back-and-forth is all part of the life. But there’s a line, and in Zephyr, it’s important we keep things clean while still having fun.

**🗣️ In-Character Trash Talk**

* Shit talking is permitted, as long as it stays in-character and makes sense within the RP
* If you're beefing with a crew, let the tension build — but it should be justified, not just thrown around for no reason
* Anything that crosses into OOC attacks, real-life insults, or personal digs is strictly off-limits

**🚫 Not Okay, Under Any Circumstance** 

* Sexual emotes or actions toward other players
* Purposely humiliating others with non-consensual RP or emotes (peeing on people/bodies)
* Use of racist, homophobic, or discriminatory language — even “in character”
* Targeted harassment, whether it’s through text, voice, or emotes
* Talking over dead bodys in hostiles

***
## Body Handling & Toxicity Rules
- Shooting around or over a deceased player's body with the primary intention of attracting police to that location is considered a toxicity rule breach and will be treated as such.
- Moving a deceased player's body into an area where it is intentionally likely to be discovered by police is considered a toxicity rule breach and will be treated as such.
- Protecting a deceased player's body from being revived by EMS, with the intention of causing the character to lose memory of the event, is permitted.
- Ocean dumping or otherwise disposing of a body to prevent revival is also allowed, provided it is done as part of legitimate roleplay and not solely to create a toxic experience for other players.
